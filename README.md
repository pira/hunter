# Hunter Arcade Shooter

Hunter is a fast-paced survival arena game where you kite waves of emoji monsters while juggling an arsenal of upgradable weapons. The action is rendered on a single-screen canvas and ends only when the hunter runs out of health.

## Core Loop
- **Move and survive:** Dash around the arena to avoid getting touched by monsters while lining up shots.
- **Eliminate enemies:** Use the active weapon to cut through waves of foes. Kills award score and increment a running kill counter.
- **Collect power-ups:** Temporary boosts such as rapid fire, multi-shot, invincibility, or freezing monsters drop during play. Grabbing them before they expire turns the tide of battle.
- **Level up:** Each score threshold increases the level, ramps monster speed and spawn rules, and introduces stronger variants to keep pressure high.

## Weapons & Abilities
- **Bullets:** Default ranged attack with optional rapid-fire and multi-shot modifiers.
- **Sword:** A spinning melee blade that clears space around the hunter when activated.
- **Grenades:** Lobbed explosives that erupt in area-of-effect damage and can trigger a screen-wide flash.
- **Missiles:** Slow-seeking projectiles that chase targets for high burst damage.
- **Bombs & specials:** Screen-clearing effects and explosions reward smart timing.

## Controls
- **Desktop:** Move with WASD or arrow keys, aim with the mouse, and click/space to fire. Number keys cycle the weapon wheel.
- **Mobile:** A virtual joystick handles movement while tap gestures trigger attacks, so the game remains playable on touch devices.

## Session Goals
- Chain kills to climb the scoreboard before the arena is overrun.
- Rotate weapons to handle different monster formations and resistances.
- Master power-up timing and positioning to reach higher levels and survive longer.

Stay alert, keep moving, and become the top monster hunter!
